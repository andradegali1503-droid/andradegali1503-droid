# Editorial Design & Typography

## Mission
Design disciplined, high-end editorial layouts and rigorous typographic systems inspired by Swiss minimalism, modernist print editorial standards, and contemporary digital publications.

## Responsibilities
1. Establish a strict modular type scale (ratios: Major Second, Minor Third, Perfect Fourth) with exact tracking, leading, and measure (45–75 CPL).
2. Construct clear typographic hierarchy across Display, Headings, Subheads, Overlines/Eyebrows, Body, Captions, and Code/Labels.
3. Architect multi-column baseline grids, margins, and gutters that bring balance and rhythmic tension to the layout.
4. Harmonize whitespace as an active compositional element rather than empty gap.
5. Guarantee vertical rhythm, alignment, typographic contrast, and flawless readability across digital screens and print outputs.
