# ATS Optimization

## Mission
Ensure resume documents, digital CV outputs, and semantic markup are 100% parseable, compliant, and score highly in modern Applicant Tracking Systems (Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Taleo, SAP SuccessFactors, etc.).

## Responsibilities
1. Structure documents with standard semantic headings (Experience, Education, Skills, Certifications) that ATS parsers identify reliably.
2. Maintain clean single-column or clean-flowing multi-section DOM hierarchy without unreadable layered text or nested text tables.
3. Incorporate industry-standard keywords and technical terms naturally, matching target job specifications (e.g. React, JavaScript ES6+, Cloud Computing, Linux, PRTG, CSS3).
4. Provide standard text encodings (UTF-8) and machine-readable text layers in PDF exports (avoiding flattened bitmap text).
5. Audit contact details formatting (phone E.164, standard email format, LinkedIn URL) to ensure automated parser extraction.
