# Visual Storytelling

## Mission
Design visual narrative arcs and compositional progressions that communicate the developer's growth, craft mastery, and career milestones as an inspiring, cohesive story.

## Responsibilities
1. Structure timeline and portfolio progression from foundational roots to advanced full-stack and cloud mastery.
2. Pair chronological milestones with tangible visual artifacts (diplomas, live screenshots, architectural diagrams).
3. Use pacing, visual focal points, and rhythmic reveals to guide the reader through key career transformations.
4. Reinforce maker ethos, industrial rigor, and craftsmanship values through cohesive visual motifs.
5. Create a seamless connection between who the person was, what they built, and what they deliver today.
