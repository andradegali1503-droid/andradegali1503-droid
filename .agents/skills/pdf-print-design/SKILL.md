# PDF & Print Design

## Mission
Architect clean, high-density, beautifully formatted `@media print` stylesheets and standalone PDF templates that produce flawless single-page or two-page printed resumes.

## Responsibilities
1. Strip all interactive web chrome (canvas, shaders, cursors, navbars, buttons) cleanly on print/PDF triggers.
2. Formulate pure high-contrast black-on-white or deep-navy typography optimized for laser printers and PDF parsers.
3. Manage page break controls (`break-inside: avoid`, `page-break-after: auto`) to prevent orphan headers or chopped experience cards.
4. Scale margins, line heights, and typography precisely for standard ISO A4 and US Letter page dimensions.
5. Embed clickable hyperlinks and clean contact information text directly in the generated PDF document.
