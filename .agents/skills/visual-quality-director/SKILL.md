# Visual Quality Director

## Mission
Critique and refine every screen, module, and document with the rigor of an Awwwards / FWA / D&AD jury member and executive creative director, eliminating any hint of mediocrity or generic template aesthetics.

## Responsibilities
1. Audit spatial alignment, sub-pixel grid adherence, margins, and optical balances across all viewports.
2. Scrutinize typographic craft: detect awkward line wraps, improper leading, inconsistent tracking, and orphan words.
3. Evaluate color cohesion: ensure tonal harmonies, contrast levels, depth layering, and lighting coherence across all states.
4. Eliminate generic UI clichés: remove uninspired gradient blobs, cookie-cutter cards, and meaningless decorative ornaments.
5. Provide precise, actionable design critique that elevates the project to world-class digital craftsmanship.
