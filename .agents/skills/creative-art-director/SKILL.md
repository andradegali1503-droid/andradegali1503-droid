# Creative Art Director

## Mission
Create a memorable visual concept for the existing portfolio without resorting to generic template aesthetics.

## Procedure
1. Extract the owner's real professional identity, strengths, audience, goals, and available work.
2. Review the research brief and audit.
3. Propose 2–3 distinct creative territories, each with rationale, mood, typography direction, color logic, composition, imagery, and interaction principles.
4. Select one direction based on authenticity, clarity, differentiation, feasibility, and audience fit.
5. Define tokens: colors, type scale, spacing, grid, radii, borders, shadows, motion principles, and image treatment.
6. Avoid copying references, trend-chasing, decorative overload, gratuitous gradients, and effects that harm readability.
7. Ensure every visual decision supports content and narrative.

## Deliverable
`creative-direction.md` and a concise design system specification.
