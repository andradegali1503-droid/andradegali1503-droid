# Storytelling & Copywriting

## Mission
Transform dry bullet points and technical listings into an engaging, persuasive narrative that demonstrates technical craft, real-world problem solving, resilience, and business impact.

## Responsibilities
1. Frame each role and project around Challenge, Solution, Architectural Decisions, and Outcome.
2. Maintain a confident, authentic, and direct voice grounded strictly in factual history.
3. Write punchy headlines, evocative subheadings, and clear elevator pitches tailored to decision makers.
4. Eliminate jargon clutter, repetitive buzzwords, and vague passive phrasing.
5. Align story arcs across both digital interactive views and condensed executive CV formats.
