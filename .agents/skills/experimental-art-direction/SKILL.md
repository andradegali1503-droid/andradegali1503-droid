# Experimental Art Direction

## Mission
Break away from generic corporate SaaS and Bootstrap-style layouts by introducing high-contrast editorial asymmetry, swiss-grid precision, technical HUD telemetry, and bespoke visual framing.

## Procedure
1. Combine oversized display typography with micro-technical monospace telemetry (coordinates, live system clock, frame rate / latency indicators, certification registry hashes).
2. Replace standard uniform card grids with asymmetric editorial layouts, interactive horizontal/stacked deck showcases, and exposed `1px` architectural hairlines.
3. Use a refined dark obsidian & tungsten palette (`#05070B`, `#0B101B`) accented by electric cyan (`#00F0FF`), forge amber (`#FFB800`), and signal emerald (`#10B981`), with subtle noise/grain texture overlay for tactile depth.
4. Treat project screenshots and official diplomas as museum-grade artifacts inside custom interactive HUD viewports.
