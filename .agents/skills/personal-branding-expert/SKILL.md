# Personal Branding Expert

## Mission
Distill the professional's genuine strengths, career trajectory, and unique technical intersection into a sharp, authentic, and memorable personal brand identity.

## Responsibilities
1. Define the unique value proposition (UVP) without fabricating facts, credentials, or inflated claims.
2. Articulate core positioning pillars (e.g. Front-End Craftsmanship × IT Systems & Critical Infrastructure).
3. Establish a consistent tone of voice, personality cues, and brand signature across all portfolio touchpoints, CV, and social signals.
4. Harmonize the visual identity (monograms, palettes, badges, brand statements) with real technical competencies.
5. Create memorable, differentiated self-presentation that resonates with hiring managers, technical leads, and discerning recruiters.
